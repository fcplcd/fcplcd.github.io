Assigments ​

The course assignments serve to reinforce the lecture material and to acquire practical knowledge of the Rocq theorem prover. You will generally have two weeks to complete each assignment sheet. The exercise slot in the first week is meant for discussing the assignment and clarifying any problems you run into while working on it. The deadline is typically exactly one day before the second exercise slot, so that we can use the slot in the second week to go through the solution together.

You can submit your solutions by emailing them to us (fcplcd@barkhauseninstitut.org) by the specified due date. You are not required to submit the assignments in order to take the exam. However, we will take your solutions into account when designing your exam questions. This means, for example, that you are more likely to receive specific programming tasks in the exam if we cannot tell from your submissions whether you have learned to program in Rocq.
